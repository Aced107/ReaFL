# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-29)

- First release.
- Tools: Default (`1`) and Mute (`T`), with a ReaFL toolbar that lights the active tool.
- Mute tool: left click toggles an item's mute without changing selection; right click deletes the item.
  A double click toggles twice (no net change) and never opens REAPER's double-click action.
- Setup and Uninstall actions for shortcuts, toolbar and startup (REAPER always starts in Default).
